Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

TogiClipper is a private desktop application used only by the TogiMomentsTV team. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Information we access

  • TikTok: when the TogiMomentsTV team connects its own TikTok account, TogiClipper receives the basic account information TikTok provides (account ID and display name) and an access token that lets it send approved video drafts to that account.
  • Videos: the clips TogiClipper sends are made by the team from content it is authorized to use.

How it is used and stored

  • The information is used only to send approved drafts to the TogiMomentsTV TikTok account and to show whether a send succeeded.
  • Everything is stored only on the team's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • TogiClipper does not access other people's TikTok accounts or data.

Removing access

The account owner can disconnect TogiClipper at any time in the TikTok app's settings (security and permissions, apps). Stored tokens can be deleted from the computer at any time.
