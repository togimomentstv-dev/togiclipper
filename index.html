TogiClipper

TogiClipper is a private desktop tool used by the team behind the TogiMomentsTV channel. It turns long videos from the creator Togi, which the team is authorized to use, into short vertical clips, and helps the team review them before posting.

What it does

  • Finds moments in authorized long videos and edits them into vertical clips with captions.
  • Shows every clip in a review screen. A person must approve each clip before it can be posted.
  • For TikTok: after approval, a person can choose to send the approved clip to the TogiMomentsTV TikTok account as a draft. The person then finishes and publishes the post in the TikTok app.

TogiClipper never posts anything without a person approving it, and it only works with the TogiMomentsTV accounts.
